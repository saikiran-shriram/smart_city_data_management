# Smart Solapur – Smart City Data Management System

A Smart City management project designed to manage different municipal services of Solapur through three independent modules, each using a different database technology.

## Project Modules

### 1. Citizen Registration and Property Management (MySQL)
**Database:** MySQL

- Citizen registration and management
- Property registration and ownership management
- Property tax and utility bill generation
- Payment tracking and management
- Rental agreement management

### 2. Citizen Complaints and Grievance Management (MongoDB)
**Database:** MongoDB

- Citizen complaint registration
- Complaint tracking and management
- Grievance status updates
- Complaint resolution management

### 3. Public Transportation Management (Neo4j)
**Database:** Neo4j

- Public transportation information management
- Transport route management
- Route and connectivity analysis
- Transportation network representation

The three modules are currently being developed independently. Each module has its own database and functionality.

The next development phase will focus on integrating all three modules into a unified interface.
